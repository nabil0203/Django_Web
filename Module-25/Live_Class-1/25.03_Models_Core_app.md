# 📊Model  List

1️⃣ Category  
2️⃣ Course  
3️⃣ Lesson  
4️⃣ Material  
5️⃣ Enrollment  
6️⃣ QuestionAnswer  
7️⃣ User (from users_app)




--------------------



### 1️⃣ Category & Course
- 1 Category can have Multiple Courses  
- 1 Course belongs to only 1 Category  
- **Category & Course == 1 to Many**

----------------------------

### 2️⃣ User (Instructor) & Course
- 1 User (Instructor) can create Multiple Courses  
- 1 Course belongs to only 1 Instructor  
- **User & Course == 1 to Many**

----------------------------

### 3️⃣ Course & Lesson
- 1 Course can have Multiple Lessons  
- Each Lesson belongs to only 1 Course  
- **Course & Lesson == 1 to Many**

----------------------------

### 4️⃣ Course & Material
- 1 Course can have Multiple Materials  
- Each Material belongs to only 1 Course  
- **Course & Material == 1 to Many**

----------------------------

### 5️⃣ User (Student) & Enrollment
- 1 User (Student) can enroll in Multiple Courses  
- Each Enrollment belongs to only 1 User  
- **User & Enrollment == 1 to Many**

----------------------------

### 6️⃣ Course & Enrollment
- 1 Course can have Multiple Enrollments  
- Each Enrollment belongs to only 1 Course  
- **Course & Enrollment == 1 to Many**

----------------------------

### 7️⃣ User & QuestionAnswer
- 1 User can create Multiple Questions/Answers  
- Each QuestionAnswer belongs to only 1 User  
- **User & QuestionAnswer == 1 to Many**

----------------------------

### 8️⃣ Lesson & QuestionAnswer
- 1 Lesson can have Multiple Questions/Answers  
- Each QuestionAnswer belongs to only 1 Lesson  
- **Lesson & QuestionAnswer == 1 to Many**
